I recently got an [XTEINK X4 Pro eReader](https://www.xteink.com/products/xteink-x4-pro-pocket-ereader) and immediately put  [CrossPoint Reader Firmware](https://crosspointreader.com/) on it. It's a much better experience than the stock firmware.

More software for the X4: I found [this Chrome extension and iPhone app](https://www.chapiware.com/send-to-x4/) that can send articles directly to your X4. I'm hoping this helps me get through my read later backlog.

Craig Mod continues to be [software bonkers](https://craigmod.com/essays/software_bonkers/) and created an [interactive log and map](https://craigmod.com/walks/) of all the walks he's been on around the world.

This [album of old control rooms](https://www.flickr.com/groups/419512@N22/pool/) is mesmerizing. There's so much detail in each of these photos. It's brain-bending to think about all the work that went into the design and functionality of some of these.

You can [destroy any website](https://destroy.spritefusion.com/). It reminds me of a very old application, I think on Windows 3.1 or Windows 95, that let you blow up your desktop. Pure joy.
